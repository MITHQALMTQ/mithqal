## Purpose

This roadmap distinguishes — at all times — between:

1. what MITHQAL **is today** (the current operating entity),
2. what MITHQAL **plans to become** commercially, and
3. the **target constitutional architecture** that governs reserves independently
   of any commercial entity.

No stage may be conflated with another. No planned entity may be presented as
existing. See [`institutional-principles.md`](../legal/institutional-principles.md)
for the canonical wording.

## Stage 3 — TARGET Constitutional Architecture

> **LABEL: TARGET.** This is the institutional destination. It does **not**
> exist today and is not currently operating.

The target architecture separates **commercial governance** from
**constitutional governance**. The two sides are independent. The Constitution
governs the Institution; the commercial companies execute operations.

### Target — Commercial side

```
Founder → MITHQAL Holding Company (planned) → [Operations Ltd. (planned) | Markets Ltd. (planned)]
```

### Target — Constitutional side

```
                MITHQAL Constitution
                       │
           ┌───────────┴───────────┐
           │                       │
           ▼                       ▼
  Constitutional Foundation   Custodian Banks
     (planned)                 (approved institutions)
           │                       │
  monitors constitutional        │
  compliance                    ▼
                          Reserve Assets
                          (Gold · Silver · Cash
                           · Sukuk · Other)
```

The Foundation and Custodian Banks are **separate roles** under the Constitution.
The Foundation monitors constitutional compliance — it does not manage banks.
Banks custody assets — they do not govern.

### Independence rules (target state — never violated)

- **NEVER** connect the Foundation underneath the Holding Company.
- **NEVER** connect reserves underneath Operations.
- **NEVER** connect reserves underneath Markets.
- The Foundation exists to **protect the Constitution** — it does **not**:
  operate commercially, own reserves, custody reserves, own companies, or
  perform operations.
- Constitutional principles governing organizational architecture are
  **immutable except through constitutional amendment**. Legal entity names
  may evolve; jurisdictions may change; corporate structures may adapt.
  **Constitutional principles may not.**

### Canonical Reserve Independence Wording

> "Reserve assets are held in segregated custody under the Constitutional
> Reserve Framework through approved custodian institutions for the exclusive
> benefit of the MITHQAL reserve system. They are never operating assets and
> never corporate assets of JOZOUR LLC or any future operating entity."
