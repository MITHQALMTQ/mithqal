# Custodian Eligibility Matrix

**Author:** Chief Constitutional Engineer / Institutional Infrastructure Architect (acting in concert)
**Status:** CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT PROPOSAL — pending Council ratification
**Authority:** §4 of `docs/blueprint/custody-framework-v2.md`

## Purpose

This matrix operationalizes the 12 custodian eligibility criteria defined in §4 of the Mithqal Constitutional Custody Framework v2.0. Every custodian — at any tier (Tier 1 official-sector, Tier 2 regulated bank, Tier 3 specialized vault, Tier 4 contingency) — must satisfy **all 12 criteria** before approval and maintain satisfaction throughout the custodian relationship.

The matrix is a verification tool for the **Custodian Eligibility Committee** (to be established under the Foundation's oversight). It is not a self-certification checklist — every criterion must be independently verified by a qualified third party (legal opinion, audit report, regulatory filing, or equivalent evidence).

## The 12 Eligibility Criteria

| # | Criterion | Requirement | Tier 1 (Official-Sector) | Tier 2 (Regulated Bank) | Tier 3 (Specialized Vault) | Tier 4 (Contingency) | Verification Method | Evidence Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Legal authority** | The institution must have statutory authority to provide custody services to MITHQAL. | ✅ Required — central bank must have statutory authority to custody assets for non-official entities (many central banks exclude private-sector entities) | ✅ Required — bank must hold a banking license that permits custody services | ✅ Required — vault operator must have legal authority to store precious metals for third parties | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Legal opinion from a qualified law firm in the custodian's home jurisdiction | Legal opinion letter (signed, on firm letterhead) |
| **2** | **Regulatory authorization** | The institution must hold all required licenses, registrations, and regulatory approvals to operate as a custodian. | ✅ Required — central bank operates under its statutory mandate (no separate license needed, but mandate must permit the service) | ✅ Required — banking license + custody license (if separately required) + securities custodian license (if applicable) | ✅ Required — vault operator license + precious-metals dealer license (if applicable) + LBMA registration for bullion custodians | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Regulatory license verification; ongoing monitoring of license status | Regulator confirmation letter; license certificate; LBMA membership confirmation |
| **3** | **Institutional standing** | The institution must be a recognized institutional custodian. | ✅ Required — central bank, monetary authority, BIS-type institution, or other official-sector institution | ✅ Required — systemically important bank; minimum credit rating A- (Fitch/S&P) or A3 (Moody's) | ✅ Required — LBMA-registered vault operator for bullion; recognized specialized custodian for non-bullion | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Independent industry ranking; credit rating; LBMA registration | Fitch/S&P/Moody's rating report; LBMA member directory entry; industry ranking publication |
| **4** | **Segregation capability** | The institution must be able to hold MITHQAL reserve assets in segregated, bankruptcy-remote accounts. | ✅ Required — allocated/earmarked custody (BIS model: depositor retains beneficial ownership, specific bars identified) | ✅ Required — segregated custody account, bankruptcy-remote from bank's own assets | ✅ Required — allocated custody with individually identifiable bars | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Custody agreement must specify allocated, segregated custody; legal opinion confirming bankruptcy-remote status | Executed custody agreement (segregation clauses); legal opinion on bankruptcy-remote status |
| **5** | **Operational resilience** | The institution must demonstrate operational resilience (BCP, DR, cyber security, physical security). | ✅ Required — central bank-grade operational resilience | ✅ Required — SOC 2 Type II report (or equivalent); ISO 27001 certification | ✅ Required — SOC 2 Type II; physical security certification (UL, LPS 1175, or equivalent for vaults) | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | SOC 2 Type II report; ISO 27001 certificate; independent operational resilience assessment | SOC 2 Type II report (current); ISO 27001 certificate; physical security audit report |
| **6** | **Auditability** | The institution must permit independent audit of MITHQAL reserve assets held in its custody. | ✅ Required — central bank must permit audit (may be subject to confidentiality restrictions) | ✅ Required — quarterly independent audit rights | ✅ Required — quarterly physical bar count + audit rights | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Custody agreement must grant MITHQAL (and its independent auditors) audit rights; quarterly audit cycle | Executed custody agreement (audit rights clauses); auditor engagement letter |
| **7** | **AML/CFT compliance** | The institution must comply with all applicable anti-money-laundering and counter-financing-of-terrorism requirements. | ✅ Required — central bank AML/CFT framework | ✅ Required — bank AML/CFT program (FATF-aligned) | ✅ Required — vault operator AML/CFT program | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | AML/CFT policy review; ongoing sanctions screening | AML/CFT policy document; FATF compliance assessment; sanctions screening procedures |
| **8** | **Sanctions compliance** | The institution must implement sanctions screening consistent with MITHQAL's sanctions policy. | ✅ Required — central bank sanctions framework | ✅ Required — OFAC/UN/EU sanctions screening | ✅ Required — sanctions screening for bullion transactions | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Sanctions screening procedures review; OFAC/UN/EU sanctions list screening | Sanctions screening procedures document; screening tool vendor confirmation; test results |
| **9** | **Reporting capability** | The institution must be able to report holdings, transactions, and custody attestations to MITHQAL daily. | ✅ Required — central bank reporting (may be SWIFT or proprietary) | ✅ Required — daily SWIFT MT54x/ISO 20022 reporting | ✅ Required — daily bar-level holdings report | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Custody agreement must specify daily reporting; API or SWIFT MT54x/ISO 20022 capability | Executed custody agreement (reporting clauses); API documentation or SWIFT capability confirmation |
| **10** | **Disaster recovery** | The institution must have a documented and tested disaster recovery plan. | ✅ Required — central bank DR plan | ✅ Required — bank DR plan (annual test) | ✅ Required — vault DR plan (physical recovery + cyber) | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | DR plan review; annual DR test results | DR plan document; most recent DR test report |
| **11** | **Independent verification** | The institution must permit independent third-party verification of reserve assets. | ✅ Required — central bank must permit independent verification (subject to confidentiality) | ✅ Required — annual independent verification | ✅ Required — annual physical bar count by independent verifier | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Custody agreement must grant MITHQAL (and its independent verifiers) verification rights; annual physical bar count for bullion | Executed custody agreement (verification rights clauses); independent verifier engagement letter |
| **12** | **Contractual enforceability** | The custody arrangement must be governed by a legally enforceable contract including all constitutional requirements. | ✅ Required — custody agreement (may be a central-bank standard form; constitutional requirements must be incorporated by reference) | ✅ Required — bilateral custody agreement with all constitutional clauses | ✅ Required — vault agreement with all constitutional clauses | ✅ Required — same as primary tier | Legal opinion confirming enforceability under the governing law | Executed custody agreement; legal opinion on enforceability |

## Tier-Specific Additional Requirements

In addition to the 12 universal criteria, each tier has specific requirements:

### Tier 1 — Official-Sector Custodian (Additional)

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **Statutory authority for non-official entities** | The central bank must have statutory authority to custody assets for entities that are not themselves central banks or official international institutions. (The NY Fed explicitly excludes private-sector entities; the Bank of England can consider certain commercial firms under specific criteria.) |
| **No conflict with monetary policy** | The custody arrangement must not conflict with the central bank's monetary policy mandate. |
| **Political risk assessment** | An independent political risk assessment must be conducted for the jurisdiction. |
| **Diplomatic engagement** | Engagement with the central bank must be coordinated with MITHQAL's institutional diplomacy track (not a direct commercial approach). |

### Tier 2 — Regulated Bank Custodian (Additional)

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **Minimum credit rating** | A- (Fitch/S&P) or A3 (Moody's); AA- or higher preferred for primary custodians. |
| **Systemic importance** | The bank should be designated as a systemically important financial institution (SIFI) by its home regulator, OR be a major institutional custodian with demonstrated scale. |
| **Custody business line** | The bank must have a dedicated custody business line (not incidental custody). |
| **Sub-custodian disclosure** | The bank must disclose any sub-custodian arrangements and obtain MITHQAL consent for sub-custody. |

### Tier 3 — Specialized Vault Custodian (Additional)

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **LBMA registration** | For bullion custody, the vault operator must be LBMA-registered (or equivalent industry standard). |
| **Physical security certification** | The vault must hold a recognized physical security certification (UL Class 3, LPS 1175 SR4, or equivalent). |
| **Insurance** | The vault must maintain comprehensive insurance covering the full replacement value of stored assets (all-risk, with explicit coverage for theft, fire, flood, earthquake, and terrorism). |
| **Bar-level accounting** | The vault must maintain bar-level accounting (serial number, weight, purity, assay, vault location, compartment) for every bar. |
| **No rehypothecation** | The vault agreement must explicitly prohibit rehypothecation, lending, or pledging of stored bars. |

### Tier 4 — Contingency Custodian (Additional)

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pre-approval** | The contingency custodian must be pre-approved by the Constitutional Council before activation. |
| **Activation readiness** | The contingency custodian must be able to accept custody within 7 days of activation (per the §Article VIII DR RTO). |
| **Capacity** | The contingency custodian must have sufficient capacity to accept the reserves being transferred (minimum 25% of total reserve value). |
| **No active competing mandate** | The contingency custodian must not be actively holding MITHQAL reserves at the time of activation (to avoid concentration). |

## Verification Cycle

| Criterion | Verification Frequency |
|---|---|
| 1. Legal authority | At onboarding; re-verified every 3 years or upon change in law |
| 2. Regulatory authorization | At onboarding; continuous monitoring (regulator database) |
| 3. Institutional standing | At onboarding; annual review (rating changes) |
| 4. Segregation capability | At onboarding (legal opinion); annual review (custody agreement audit) |
| 5. Operational resilience | At onboarding; annual review (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 renewal) |
| 6. Auditability | At onboarding; quarterly (audit cycle) |
| 7. AML/CFT compliance | At onboarding; annual review |
| 8. Sanctions compliance | At onboarding; continuous monitoring (daily sanctions screening) |
| 9. Reporting capability | At onboarding; daily (reporting receipt verification) |
| 10. Disaster recovery | At onboarding; annual review (DR test results) |
| 11. Independent verification | At onboarding; annual (physical bar count for bullion; account confirmation for cash) |
| 12. Contractual enforceability | At onboarding (legal opinion); review at every contract renewal |

## Eligibility Decision Matrix

| Outcome | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **All 12 criteria met** | Custodian is eligible | Proceed to Custodian Eligibility Committee approval; execute custody agreement |
| ⚠️ **11 of 12 criteria met** | Custodian is conditionally eligible | Remediation plan required; conditional approval with timeline for compliance |
| ❌ **≤10 of 12 criteria met** | Custodian is not eligible | Reject application; do not execute custody agreement |

### Critical Rule

> **No custodian may be approved without meeting all 12 criteria.** Tier classification (Tier 1/2/3/4) expresses institutional preference, not a relaxation of eligibility requirements. A central bank (Tier 1) must satisfy the same criteria as a regulated bank (Tier 2) or a specialized vault (Tier 3).

---

## Evidence Retention

All eligibility verification evidence must be retained for the duration of the custodian relationship + 10 years (per the constitutional record-retention requirement). Evidence is stored in the MITHQAL data room under NDA; certain evidence (legal opinions, audit reports) may be restricted to the Custodian Eligibility Committee and the independent auditors.
