- **API verification:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}"` for 16 endpoints; sample response bodies for `/api/transparency` and `/api/v24.2.1/tgbs`.
- **View-switcher click test:** snapshot interactive elements, click 4 buttons sequentially, verify body content changes.
