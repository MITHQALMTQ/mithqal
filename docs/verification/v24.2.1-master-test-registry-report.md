- **8 of 60 FAILs (13%) are UNRESOLVED** — ERTF failure/delay (10) + Weekend gap (5) + small
  gold shock (3). These require operational changes (ERTF operationalization, ceiling raise).

- **4 of 60 FAILs (7%) are at the boundary** — these are the §46 TEST A/C/D demonstrations that
  the 102% ceiling is not immune to small losses. They are honest expected failures per the
  directive's deterministic-test design.

**Aggregate failure rate is high but structurally interpretable:** the system PASSES all
invariant tests (anti-double-counting 32/32, deterministic proofs 5/5, MC reproducibility 1/1,
challenger confirmation 4/5, model validation 7/10) and FAILs only in severe stress scenarios
