## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document evaluates MITHQAL's institutional readiness from the perspective of 9 stakeholder types across 10 jurisdictions. The finding: **MITHQAL is technically ready but NOT institutionally ready.** No jurisdiction has approved it. Reserves are unverified. AML/KYC is missing. The path to institutional readiness requires 6-12 months of regulatory engagement and operational hardening.

## 5. INSTITUTIONAL READINESS ROADMAP

### Phase 1: Operational hardening (Months 1-3)
- Deploy MTQ, Mint, Algorithm contracts
- Add 3rd gold oracle source
- Add multi-oracle for silver and FX
- Implement stablecoin depeg monitoring
- Implement AML/KYC for mint/redeem
- Implement OFAC sanctions screening

### Phase 2: Custody & verification (Months 3-6)
- Engage qualified custodians (JP Morgan, BNY, Brink's, etc.)
- Transfer reserves to custodian accounts
- Build attestation integration
- Achieve Level 2 (custodian-attested) for all reserves
- Publish PoR report

### Phase 3: Governance & compliance (Months 4-6)
- Form Monetary Council (7 members)
- Form Sharia supervisory board
- Formalize incident response and disaster recovery
- Implement key rotation and HSM

### Phase 4: Regulatory engagement (Months 6-12)
- Engage regulators in Switzerland, UAE, Singapore (GREEN jurisdictions)
- Obtain regulatory opinion or license
- Engage Big 4 auditor for annual audit
- Achieve Level 3 (independently audited)

### Phase 5: Track record (Ongoing, 12+ months)
- Operate in SHADOW mode for 6+ months
- Demonstrate stress resilience
- Build adoption network
- Achieve Level 4 (continuous verification) where possible

## 6. CONCLUSION

MITHQAL is **technically functional but NOT institutionally ready.** The overall institutional readiness score is **1.8/10** — far below the threshold for mainnet launch.

The path to institutional readiness requires:
1. **6 months** of operational hardening (AML/KYC, sanctions, oracle hardening, contract deployment)
2. **6 months** of custody and verification (custodian engagement, attestation, PoR)
3. **6-12 months** of regulatory engagement (Switzerland, UAE, Singapore first)
4. **12+ months** of track record (SHADOW mode operation)

**Total time to institutional readiness: 12-18 months after management approval.**

**No claim of regulatory approval or institutional credibility can be made until this roadmap is completed.**
