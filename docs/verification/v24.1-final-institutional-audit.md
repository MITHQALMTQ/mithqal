### 2.3 Key Stress Test Findings

1. **The portfolio's dominant solvency risk is fiat/FX, not bullion.** Gold -50% costs only 8.7pp; USD +20% costs 12pp. At 15% weight, gold barely moves RR. Fiat is 76.5% of the risk.

2. **PAR redemption is RR-accretive when over-collateralized.** Redemption itself is NOT the danger; the danger is redemption combined with a shock that pushes RR toward/below 1 (scenarios 18, 19).

3. **Gold is never liquidated up to 50% redemption.** Non-gold liquid sleeve ≈ 80% of portfolio ≈ $52.8M > $27M (50% payout). The "gold last" / Exhaustion Certificate protection holds in practice. ✅ Genuine strength.

4. **The 120% buffer is exactly a 20% systematic cushion.** A correlated -20% across all assets (correlation→1) yields 96% — FAIL. The break-even correlated systematic shock is only ~-16.7%. Diversification is the ONLY systematic defense; if it fails, so does solvency.

5. **Custodian concentration cap (≤25%) is not calibrated to solvency.** A single custodian at the 25% cap with 100% LGD → RR = 90% (FAIL). With 50% LGD → 105% (barely pass). No LGD/recovery assumption is documented.

### Is v24.1 mathematically sound?

No legal opinions are obtained. No MiCA classification. No VARA/CBUAE license. No MAS/HKMA/JFSA engagement. Middle East is PARTIAL (correct CBDC positioning, but no license).

Central-bank trust must be earned through demonstrated operational excellence over years, not claimed in a blueprint.

**NOT YET.** The tokenomics are sound (dynamic supply, bounded minting, never-pausable burn, policy-level fees). The architecture supports institutional settlement (fixed PAR, multi-currency reserves, Article X protection, 120% buffer, prefunded redemption liquidity). ### Is v24.1 the "most stable, trusted" architecture?
