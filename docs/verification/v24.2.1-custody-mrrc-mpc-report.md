### §41 Recommended Optimal λ

**Recommended optimal λ = 0.5** (composite score = 6.329965)

Selection criterion: `max(composite_score)` where:
```
composite_score = (StressRR_mean / 10)
                  − (CVaR_99 / R_a × 100) × 0.1
                  − (exec_cost / R_a × 100) × 5
```
(lower CVaR is better → subtracted; higher StressRR is better → added; lower execution cost is better → subtracted, weighted 5× to penalise churn)

### §41 Key Findings

- **λ=0.50 (most aggressive shift to W_stress):** lowest CVaR ($21,838,214), but highest execution cost ($1,620) and lifecycle cost ($2,430).
- **Trade-off:** as λ decreases, the portfolio shifts toward cash/HQLA, reducing tail risk but incurring turnover. The optimal λ balances these.
- **Per §41 directive:** 'Do not assume 70/30 permanently.' The recommended λ here is data-driven, not hard-coded.
