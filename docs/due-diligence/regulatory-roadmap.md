**Constitutional basis:** Blueprint Part 2 Article XVII §6 (Independent Review Policy) and §10 (Legal Validation Framework); Part 1 Article XI (Regulatory Adaptability)

## Overview

The MITHQAL Regulatory Roadmap defines the Institution's engagement plan with regulators, central banks, and international standard-setting bodies. It complements the [Legal Roadmap](legal-roadmap.md) (which establishes the legal classification per jurisdiction) and the [Institutional Roadmap](institutional-roadmap.md) (which establishes the path to institutional credibility).

**Critical principle:** Engagement with a regulator does **not** constitute regulatory approval. The Institution shall not imply, suggest, or state that any regulator has approved, endorsed, or licensed MITHQAL where only engagement has occurred. Status is reported as **PENDING EXTERNAL VALIDATION** until a formal regulatory determination is obtained.

## Engagement Tiers

The Institution engages with regulators across three tiers:

### Tier A — Domestic Regulators (per operating jurisdiction)

| Regulator Type | Role |
|----------------|------|
| Central bank | Monetary / systemic risk perspective; reserve framework review |
| Securities regulator | Securities law classification |
| Financial intelligence unit | AML/CFT compliance |
| Tax authority | Tax treatment |
| State / provincial regulator | State-level licensing (where applicable) |

### Tier B — Cross-Border / Standard-Setting Bodies

| Body | Engagement |
|------|------------|
| Financial Action Task Force (FATF) | AML/CFT standards alignment |
| Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) | Banking / capital treatment of crypto exposures |
| International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) | Securities regulation standards |
| Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI) | Payment and settlement infrastructure standards |
| Bank for International Settlements (BIS) | Infrastructure review (Article XVII §6) |

### Tier C — Sovereign Reviewers

| Reviewer | Engagement |
|----------|------------|
| Central banks (institutional clients) | Technical review for reserve diversification |
| Sovereign wealth funds | Institutional due diligence |
| Multilateral development banks | Settlement infrastructure review |

## Engagement Principles

1. **Transparency-first** — proactively present the full MITHQAL framework; no selective disclosure
2. **Voluntary engagement** — engage even where not legally required, to establish good faith
3. **Documented** — every engagement is documented in the Evidence Ledger with date, attendees, topics, and any feedback received
4. **No-approval representation** — engagement is reported as "PENDING EXTERNAL VALIDATION" until formal approval is obtained (where approval is sought)
5. **Confidentiality balanced with transparency** — engagement details are confidential where required, but the existence of engagement is publicly disclosed
6. **No special treatment sought** — the Institution does not seek exemptions or special treatment unavailable to comparable institutions

## Engagement Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Engagement (Q3 2026)

- Complete [Legal Roadmap](legal-roadmap.md) Stages 1–3 (Jurisdiction Analysis, External Counsel, Legal Opinion)
- Prepare the [Due Diligence Data Room](README.md) for regulator review
- Prepare jurisdiction-specific briefing materials
- Identify regulator points of contact (where possible)

### Phase 2 — Initial Engagement (Q4 2026)

- Initiate informal engagement with Tier A regulators in priority jurisdictions (United States, European Union, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, Singapore)
- Present the MITHQAL framework
- Seek informal feedback on classification and licensing requirements
- Document each engagement in the Evidence Ledger

### Phase 3 — Licensing Applications (2027)

- Where Stage 5 of the [Legal Roadmap](legal-roadmap.md) determines licensing is required, prepare and submit license applications
- Track application status publicly
- Status remains "PENDING EXTERNAL VALIDATION" until license is granted

### Phase 4 — Ongoing Engagement (continuous)

- Quarterly engagement updates with engaged regulators
- Annual review of regulatory developments affecting MITHQAL
- Re-engagement when material changes occur (e.g., new reserve tier, new oracle family, new operating jurisdiction)

### Phase 5 — BIS / Cross-Border Review (2027–2028)

- Engage BIS for infrastructure review (per Article XVII §6)
- Engage FATF for AML/CFT alignment review
- Engage CPMI for settlement infrastructure review
- Engage BCBS for banking-sector exposure review

## Engagement Status Disclosure

Per Article XVII §1 (External Assurance Policy), the Institution publicly discloses, in the [Evidence Ledger](../evidence/EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md), the current status of each regulator engagement:

- **Not Engaged** — no engagement initiated
- **Initial Engagement** — introductory meeting(s) held
- **Substantive Engagement** — substantive review underway
- **Application Submitted** — formal application submitted (where licensing required)
- **Approved** — formal approval obtained (where sought) — *no current engagements at this status*
- **Withdrawn** — application withdrawn or jurisdiction not pursued

**No claim of "Approved" status shall be made unless and until formal written approval is received from the regulator.**

---

## What This Roadmap Does NOT Promise

1. **No promise of regulatory approval** — engagement is not approval
2. **No promise of a specific classification** — classification is determined by the regulator, not the Institution
3. **No promise of operation in any specific jurisdiction** — the Institution may elect not to operate in jurisdictions whose requirements conflict with the Constitution
4. **No promise of a specific timeline** — regulatory timelines are largely outside the Institution's control
5. **No implication of endorsement** — even where engagement is positive, no endorsement is implied
