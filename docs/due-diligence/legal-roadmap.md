# MITHQAL — Legal Roadmap

**Document status:** ✅ AVAILABLE
**Last updated:** 2026-07-19
**Constitutional basis:** Blueprint Part 2 Article XVII §10 (Legal Validation Framework); Part 1 Article XI (Regulatory Adaptability)

---

## Overview

The MITHQAL Legal Validation Framework is a six-stage programme. **Each stage must be completed before the next.** No stage may be skipped. **No public statement shall imply regulatory approval where only legal review has occurred.**

The six stages correspond to Article XVII §10:

| Stage | Name | Status |
|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | Jurisdiction Analysis | IN PROGRESS |
| 2 | External Counsel | IN PROGRESS |
| 3 | Legal Opinion | PENDING |
| 4 | Regulatory Engagement | PENDING |
| 5 | Licensing Assessment | PENDING |
| 6 | Constitutional Review | PENDING |

**Evidence classification per Article XVII §2.** Each stage's artifact is retained permanently in the Evidence Ledger.

---

## Stage 1 — Jurisdiction Analysis

**Owner:** Constitutional Council / Legal Committee
**Status:** IN PROGRESS

### Scope

Analysis of the regulatory classification of MTQ in each operating jurisdiction. MTQ may be classified variously as:

- **Digital commodity** (e.g., CFTC view of BTC/ETH in the United States)
- **Digital asset / virtual asset** (e.g., EU MiCA framework)
- **Currency / electronic money** (e.g., certain central bank regimes)
- **Security** (unlikely, but analyzed for completeness)
- **Stored value** (e.g., certain state money-transmitter regimes)
- **Payment instrument** (e.g., certain payment services regulations)

### Initial Target Jurisdictions

1. United States (federal — CFTC, SEC, FinCEN; state — NYDFS, Wyoming, others)
2. European Union (MiCA framework)
3. United Kingdom (FCA)
4. United Arab Emirates (ADGM, VARA)
5. Singapore (MAS)
6. Switzerland (FINMA)
7. Saudi Arabia (SAMA)
8. Bahrain (CBB)
9. Malaysia (SC, BNM)

### Deliverable

Jurisdiction Analysis Report — for each jurisdiction, the report identifies: (a) likely classification(s) of MTQ; (b) applicable regulator(s); (c) licensing requirements (if any); (d) ongoing compliance obligations; (e) tax treatment; (f) Sharia governance recognition.

### Next Milestone

Complete initial Jurisdiction Analysis Report for top 5 priority jurisdictions.

### Blocking Items

- Selection of priority jurisdictions
- Confirmation of operating model (which affects classification)

## Stage 2 — External Counsel

**Owner:** Constitutional Council / Legal Committee
**Status:** IN PROGRESS

### Scope

Engagement of external legal counsel in each operating jurisdiction to provide regulatory classification opinions. External counsel shall be:

- **Independent** — not affiliated with the Institution
- **Qualified** — recognized expertise in digital asset / financial regulation in the relevant jurisdiction
- **Senior** — partner-level review

### Engagement Criteria

- Prior experience with digital asset classification
- Prior experience with central bank / regulator engagement
- Sharia finance expertise (for jurisdictions where Sharia compliance is relevant)
- No conflicts of interest with MITHQAL or its competitors

### Deliverable

Engagement letters with each external counsel firm; scope of work defined; deliverables and timelines agreed.

### Next Milestone

Engage counsel in 5 priority jurisdictions.

### Blocking Items

- Stage 1 Jurisdiction Analysis (must inform scope of engagement)
- Engagement budget approval

---

## Stage 3 — Legal Opinion

**Owner:** External Counsel (under Constitutional Council oversight)
**Status:** PENDING

### Scope

Receipt of written legal opinions from external counsel on:

1. **Regulatory classification of MTQ** — Is MTQ a commodity, currency, security, digital asset, stored value, or payment instrument under the laws of the jurisdiction?
2. **Reserve framework** — Does the 4-tier reserve structure comply with applicable custody, banking, and securities laws?
3. **Minting and redemption procedures** — Do minting against reserve receipt and burning against reserve release comply with applicable money transmission, payments, and securities laws?
4. **Custody arrangements** — Do the custodian relationships comply with applicable custody and banking laws?
5. **Sharia compliance** — Is the structure consistent with Sharia principles as recognized in the jurisdiction (where relevant)?
6. **Anti-money laundering (AML) / Counter-financing of terrorism (CFT)** — What AML/CFT obligations apply?
7. **Tax treatment** — What is the tax treatment of minting, holding, redeeming, and earning (where applicable) MTQ?
8. **Sanctions** — What sanctions screening obligations apply?

### Deliverable

Written legal opinion from each external counsel firm, retained permanently in the Evidence Ledger.

### Next Milestone

Receive legal opinions from counsel in 5 priority jurisdictions.

### Blocking Items

- Stage 2 engagements (must be in place before opinions can be issued)

## Stage 4 — Regulatory Engagement

**Owner:** Constitutional Council
**Status:** PENDING

### Scope

Engagement with relevant regulators in each operating jurisdiction to present the MITHQAL framework and obtain informal feedback. Regulators to engage:

- **Central bank** — monetary / systemic risk perspective
- **Securities regulator** — securities law perspective
- **Financial intelligence unit** — AML/CFT perspective
- **Tax authority** — tax treatment
- **State / provincial regulator** (where applicable)

### Engagement Approach

- **Transparency-first** — proactively present the full MITHQAL framework
- **No-registration-seeking** — initially present without seeking registration, only seeking informal feedback
- **Documented** — every engagement is documented in the Evidence Ledger

### Important Note

**Regulatory engagement does not constitute regulatory approval.** The Institution shall not imply, suggest, or state that any regulator has approved, endorsed, or licensed MITHQAL where only engagement has occurred. Status is reported as "PENDING EXTERNAL VALIDATION" until a formal regulatory determination is obtained.

### Deliverable

Engagement summaries; regulator feedback (where provided); written correspondence.

### Next Milestone

Initiate regulatory engagement in 5 priority jurisdictions following Stage 3.

### Blocking Items

- Stage 3 legal opinions (must inform engagement approach)

## Stage 5 — Licensing Assessment

**Owner:** Constitutional Council / Legal Committee
**Status:** PENDING

### Scope

Assessment of whether any license, registration, or authorization is required in each operating jurisdiction. If required, the application process is initiated.

### Possible Licensing Requirements (varies by jurisdiction)

- **Money services business (MSB) registration** (e.g., FinCEN in the United States)
- **Money transmitter license** (e.g., state-level in the United States)
- **Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) registration** (e.g., under FATF-aligned regimes)
- **Electronic money institution (EMI) license** (e.g., in the EU/UK where classified as e-money)
- **Asset-referenced token (ART) authorization** (e.g., under EU MiCA where classified as ART)
- **Custody license** (e.g., where the Institution acts as custodian)
- **Banking license** (unlikely, but analyzed for completeness)

### Deliverable

Licensing Assessment Memorandum for each jurisdiction; where licensing is required, the application is initiated and tracked.

### Important Note

**Licensing assessment does not constitute license grant.** Application submission is not approval. Status is reported as "PENDING EXTERNAL VALIDATION" until a license is granted (if required).

### Next Milestone

Complete Licensing Assessment for 5 priority jurisdictions.

### Blocking Items

- Stage 4 regulatory feedback (informs whether licensing is required)

---

## Stage 6 — Constitutional Review

**Owner:** Constitutional Council
**Status:** PENDING

### Scope

Review of the legal opinions (Stage 3), regulatory feedback (Stage 4), and licensing assessments (Stage 5) by the Constitutional Council to confirm that:

1. The Constitution remains consistent with the legal and regulatory environment in each operating jurisdiction
2. No constitutional invariant (Article I invariants; reserve ratio; bullion protection; etc.) is violated by the legal/regulatory requirements
3. If inconsistent, constitutional amendment is considered under Part 1 Article XII (Amendment Philosophy)

### Amendment Considerations

If a legal/regulatory requirement conflicts with a constitutional principle, the Constitutional Council may:

- **Operate in a different jurisdiction** (preferred) — the Institution may elect not to operate in jurisdictions whose requirements conflict with the Constitution
- **Amend the Constitution** (last resort) — only if the amendment preserves identity, invariants, stability, trust, and redeemability per Part 1 Article XII

### Deliverable

Constitutional Review Memorandum; if amendment is proposed, the amendment is processed per Part 1 Article XII.

### Next Milestone

Complete Constitutional Review following Stages 1–5.

### Blocking Items

- Stages 1–5 (all must be complete before Constitutional Review)

## Cross-Stage Commitments

1. **Sequential completion** — Each stage must be completed before the next.
2. **Evidence Ledger citation** — Every stage's artifact is retained permanently in the Evidence Ledger.
3. **Public disclosure** — The current stage of legal validation in each operating jurisdiction is publicly disclosed in this roadmap. No silent overstatement.
4. **No regulatory approval implied** — No public statement shall imply regulatory approval where only legal review, regulatory engagement, or licensing application has occurred.
5. **Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction** — Legal validation is performed jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction; the Institution shall not operate in any jurisdiction where the legal classification of MTQ has not been established by external counsel.
6. **Continuous maintenance** — Legal validation is not a one-time event; changes in law, regulation, or jurisprudence trigger re-validation.
