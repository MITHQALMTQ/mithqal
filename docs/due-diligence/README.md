# MITHQAL Due Diligence Data Room

This data room contains all documents required for institutional review by banks, regulators, sovereign wealth funds, and independent auditors.

It is the canonical source established by **Part 2, Article XVII (Institutional Assurance Framework), Section 7** of the MITHQAL Constitutional Blueprint. Every claim the Institution makes shall be supported by a document in this data room; no document may be cited that is not in this room.

The data room is publicly accessible. Confidential documents (e.g., specific custodian agreements) are listed in the index but made available under appropriate confidentiality arrangements. Outdated documents are archived, never silently deleted.

## Evidence Classification Standard

Every claim in this data room is classified under Article XVII, Section 2:

- **PROVEN** — supported by live runtime evidence (on-chain state, custodian attestation, oracle publication)
- **SUPPORTED** — supported by documentary evidence that has been independently reviewed
- **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** — some components supported, others pending
- **PENDING EXTERNAL VALIDATION** — supported by internal evidence, awaiting independent review
- **UNVERIFIED** — claim made, no evidence presented
- **FALSE** — claim demonstrated false; publicly retracted

No claim may be marked PROVEN without live runtime evidence. No public statement may imply third-party certification, audit, or regulatory approval where none exists.

## Verification Hierarchy

Per Article XVII, Section 3, material claims proceed through four stages:

1. **Internal Verification** — by the Institution's own teams
2. **Independent Mathematical Review** — by a qualified independent reviewer
3. **External Audit** — by a Big-4 audit firm or equivalent
4. **Regulatory Review** — where subject to regulatory oversight (review ≠ approval)

Each stage must be completed before the next.
