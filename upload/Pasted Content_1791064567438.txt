============================================================

- exchange graphic

- yield

stands independently of MTQ.

============================================================

============================================================

Do not create a prominent warning banner on the hero unless one already
exists in the approved design system.

Do not visually suggest production readiness through:
- "LIVE"
- green live-status lights
- "Start Trading"
- "Open Account"
- "Deposit"
- "Withdraw"
- "Send"
- "Receive"

The homepage should feel highly advanced without pretending that
external institutional gates have already been passed.
