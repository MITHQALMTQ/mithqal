# PROMPT 1 — Canonical Monetary Identity, Solvency, and Stress-RR

**Scope:** Resolve all conflicting reserve-ratio and liability definitions. Lock the canonical monetary identity.

## 1.1 Canonical PAR and Liability

The canonical monetary identity shall be:

- **PAR = $1.00** fixed USD-denominated settlement/accounting reference unit.
- Contractual redemption liability:
  \[
  L_t = S_t \times PAR
  \]
  where \(S_t\) is circulating MTQ supply.

Do **not** use:
\[
L = S \times NAV_m
\]
for the canonical contractual liability.

Market NAV may remain an accounting/reference metric only. It must not redefine contractual liability.

## 1.2 Canonical Solvency Ratio

\[
RR_t = \frac{R_{a,t}}{S_t \times PAR}
\]

Where:

- \(R_{a,t}\) = prudential adjusted reserve value
- \(S_t\) = circulating MTQ supply
- \(PAR = 1.00\)

Required thresholds:

| Threshold | Value | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| \(RR_{floor}\) | 100% | Hard solvency floor |
| \(RR_{policy}\) | 105% | Policy operating floor |
| \(RR_{strategic\ target}\) | 120% | Strategic planning target |

Document these as separate concepts. A 120% target means adjusted reserve value equals 120% of contractual redemption liabilities. It does **not** mean a 20% market decline can automatically be survived.

## 1.3 Stress-RR Definition

\[
StressRR_t(s) = \frac{R_{stress,t}(s)}{S_t(s) \times PAR}
\]

Where:

\[
R_{stress,t}(s) =
\sum_a \left[
Q_{a,t} \times P_{a,t}(s) \times (1-H_a) \times C_{a,t}(s) \times S_a(s)
\right]
\]

Definitions:

- \(Q_{a,t}\) = quantity of asset \(a\)
- \(P_{a,t}(s)\) = stressed price of asset \(a\) under scenario \(s\)
- \(H_a\) = haircut for asset \(a\)
- \(C_{a,t}(s)\) = counterparty/credit factor under scenario \(s\)
- \(S_a(s)\) = scenario-specific survival/eligibility factor; default to 1 if not specified

Stress-RR is a planning/risk metric, not a second legal solvency ratio.

Every mandatory scenario must satisfy:
\[
StressRR(s) \ge approved\ minimum
\]

Default hard stress floor:
\[
StressRR(s) \ge 100\%
\]
unless a future independently validated constitutional amendment explicitly defines a different floor.

## 1.4 Deliverables

- Updated canonical specification for \(L_t\), \(RR_t\), \(StressRR_t(s)\)
- Full list of legacy definitions to deprecate
- Search/regression test proving no legacy liability formula remains active
- Documentation of \(R_{a,t}\) composition: eligible assets, market value, haircuts, counterparty factors
- Test suite covering PAR, liability, solvency, and stress-RR calculations

- \(L_t = S_t \times PAR\) is enforced everywhere
- \(RR_t\) and \(StressRR_t(s)\) use the canonical formulas
- Thresholds 100/105/120 are separately documented and enforced
