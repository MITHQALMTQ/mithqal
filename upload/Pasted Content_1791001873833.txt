==================================================
AV. FINAL RULE — DO NOT FAKE CERTAINTY
======================================

A complete implementation audit must be able to say:

“We found evidence.”

or:

“We did not find evidence.”

It must NOT say:

“It probably exists.”

It must NOT say:

“It should have been implemented.”

It must NOT say:

“The documentation indicates it was done.”

It must NOT say:

“The latest prompt assumes it was done.”

The standard is:

REQUIREMENT
→
IMPLEMENTATION
→
TEST
→
RUNTIME
→
EVIDENCE
→
PRESERVATION
→
NO REGRESSION.

AW. FINAL OPERATING MODE
========================

After generating the forensic report:

SET:

BUILD_MODE = FROZEN

until the audit results are reviewed.

Do NOT automatically execute remediation.

Do NOT automatically restore deleted files.

Do NOT automatically change the canonical architecture.

Do NOT automatically update production status.

Do NOT automatically update institutional-validation status.

The audit must produce the truth first.

Only after the audit establishes the exact gaps should a separate remediation plan be executed.
